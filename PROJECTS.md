- **Language**: C++ (Arduino ESP32)
- **Hardware**: ESP32, SH1106 OLED, capacitive touch, buzzer
- **Features**: 16-state expression engine, 7 info pages, NTP sync, HTTPS weather, serial telemetry
- **Status**: Complete, unpublished
